About the company

A wheat seed brand rooted in the Tarai region.

Continental Agro Tarai Seeds is building a farmer-first business around quality, reliability, and disciplined distribution.

Who we are

Focused today. Ambitious for tomorrow.

The company currently manufactures wheat seeds from its plant at Mohanpur Japti, Puranpur, District Pilibhit. Its purpose is to support farmers with dependable seed quality while creating a professional channel for dealers, retailers, agri-input businesses, and future export conversations.
